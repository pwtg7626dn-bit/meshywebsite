Five reasons to partner.

01

Industry transition to generative AI

Major game development platforms including Unity, Epic, and Roblox are moving to prompt-based game development environments.

02

Difficulty creating high-quality 3D assets and animations

Existing platforms create poor art and animations resulting in "AI Slop."

03

Distribution

Unity can expose Meshy to developers directly inside a tool they already use, lowering acquisition friction.

04

Clear product fit

Meshy generates 3D assets; Unity developers need assets to build interactive experiences. The value proposition is easy to demonstrate and measure.

05

Workflow opportunity

Generative AI can speed up asset creation, but developers still need assets that can be imported, edited, optimized, and deployed.

The gap, and the fix.

Unity AI is an engine-integrated agent for game development. Meshy already ships a free Unity plugin with a one-click bridge, but it needs an individual Pro account. Seat licenses remove that friction.

Today

Each artist signs up separately, manages credits, and bridges models by hand.

With the partnership

Seat holders get Meshy generation from their Unity entitlement, with one admin and one invoice.

Result

Assets are generated, imported, refined and shipped in a single pipeline.

Three ways to partner.

A new revenue line beyond licenses.

Meshy's Unity bridge today is limited to Pro members and above. Open it to everyone else: non-enterprise Unity users buy credits or order custom art assets, generated with Meshy tech and delivered into their project.

Buy where they already shop

Credit packs and per-asset orders sold through the Unity Asset Store and the Editor.

Custom, not catalog

Users describe the exact asset they need instead of searching for something close.

Share the upside

Unity earns on every order, and Meshy earns on usage of its stack.

Unity does not publish a standalone Asset Store revenue figure. The default uses the roughly $82.7M that Unity's FY2022 10-K reports for Strategic Partnerships and Other, the line that includes the Asset Store, as a proxy. Replace it with Unity's internal number.
